- Normal WP-Cron cannot hit this window. Staging WordPress core loads `wp-load.php`, completes `do_action('plugins_loaded')`, and only then iterates ready cron jobs and calls `do_action_ref_array()`. Traffic-based cron spawning is registered later at `wp_loaded` priority 20.
