- AIOSEO registers its cleanup method on action_scheduler_after_execute at priority 1000.
- When an AIOSEO-group action finishes and the daily cleanup cache flag is absent, the method deletes all terminal AIOSEO-group/action rows and their associated Action Scheduler logs.

## Proven call chain
- WP-CLI single-action command invokes ActionScheduler::runner()->process_action(id).
- process_action fires action_scheduler_after_execute before marking the current action complete.
- AIOSEO cleanup runs on that hook, deletes logs joined to prior terminal AIOSEO actions, then deletes those prior terminal actions.
- The current action 32680 was marked complete only after the cleanup hook returned, explaining why exactly one terminal AIOSEO action remains.

## Task 1.12 anomaly resolution
- The 75 removed historical log rows were associated with prior terminal AIOSEO actions and were deleted by AIOSEO cleanup behavior.
- This was not broad queue-runner cleanup and did not indicate execution of another pending action.
- Future AIOSEO action contracts must explicitly include this terminal-action/log cleanup side effect when the daily AIOSEO cleanup marker is absent.
